Can you move into the bus lane to turn right?

You can move into the bus lane to turn right, but only where the solid line has turned into a broken one, just before the junction. Let the bus go by, change lanes and make the turn from that lane.

By ProntoTest

Top view: an orange car in the left lane of a city street, with a bus lane and its sign on the right and a bus coming along it from behind; the route leads right into a side street

A city street with two lanes in your direction: you're in the left one, and the right one is for buses. It has BUS painted on it, and there's a sign on the pavement: S-51a “Lane reserved for specific vehicles” (Carril reservado para tipos específicos de vehículos). In a block you need to turn right. For now the bus lane is marked off by a solid line, and a bus is coming up along it behind you.

What to notice

  • The bus in its own lane is moving faster than the traffic and isn't expecting you to pull in right in front of it.
  • Taxis, motorbikes or cyclists may be using the bus lane too; the symbols on the S-51a sign show who.
  • In many cities cameras on poles and on the buses themselves watch the bus lane.
  • There's sometimes a bus stop in the bus lane before the junction: the bus may be standing right where you wanted to turn.

What to do

  1. 1While the bus lane is marked off by a solid line, stay in your lane: you mustn't cross a solid line.
  2. 2Nearer the junction the line becomes a broken one, and from that point you may move into the bus lane.
  3. 3Check your right mirror and over your shoulder, put your right indicator on and, if a bus is close behind, let it go by: in this lane the right of way belongs to those it's meant for.
  4. 4Then move over and make your turn from that lane.
  5. 5Don't turn right straight from your own lane across the bus lane: that way you cut in front of whoever is going straight on in it, whether a bus, a taxi or a cyclist.
  6. 6On the broken-line stretch you may enter the lane to turn or to pull into a driveway, but not to stop, make a U-turn or overtake.
  7. 7The symbols on the S-51a “Lane reserved for specific vehicles” sign show who else may use the lane.
  8. 8If there's a plate under the sign showing hours, the restriction only applies during those hours.
  9. 9Driving in the bus lane where you're not allowed to costs 200 euros.
